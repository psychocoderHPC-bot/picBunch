\subsection{Bunch and grid configurations}
\label{sec:configs}

Every case measured in the paper is listed in
Table~\ref{tab:configs} with the bunch and the grid, and the section that
uses it. The entries are the recorded configuration values of this work: the
mid-bunch slice cases (bunches generated on a
$64\times64\times32$ grid, cell $10^{-7}$\,m, centroid $(32,32,16)$,
$\sigma=6$ cells per axis); the large-domain cases; the cost and
accuracy cases; the $\gamma$ landscape and the aspect-4 rows; the
multi-velocity cases; and the divergence case (whose independent reference is
pending, so no row is included). The generation line for the mid-bunch
bunches is
\texttt{generate\_bunch.py --model gamma --gamma G --seed 42 --grid 64 64 32
--cell-size 1e-7 1e-7 1e-7 --centroid 32 32 16 --sigma 6 6 6 --weights
uniform} (this work). Entries with no recorded value are written as
``not recorded''; nothing here is inferred. The total charge is $N$ times
$-e$ for the electron bunches with one unit weight, where $e =
1.602176634\times10^{-19}$\,C; the kinetic energy is
$(\gamma-1)\,m_ec^2$ with $m_ec^2 = 510.999$ keV.

The mid-bunch bunches are mono-energetic: the read-back records
$\gamma_{\min}=\gamma_{\max}=1$ for the $\gamma=1$ bunch and
$\gamma_{\min}=\gamma_{\max}=10$ for the $\gamma=10$ bunch.
The earlier multi-velocity cases ($48^3$, 512 sources) were
superseded by the v0.9.1 re-measurement on a $64^3$ grid with 200 sources
(fluctuation bunches, $10^{-7}$\,m cell, \texttt{hal} A30); the v0.9.1
numbers in Section~\ref{sec:multivel} replace the earlier binning-error
table. The earlier campaign bunch files were unavailable; the per-particle
sample was regenerated from the surviving driver block (seeds 100, 103) on
\texttt{rosi\_v100}; the LCG state
and draw count are identical to the campaign, and only last-bit
\texttt{libm} differences between hosts are possible, so the sampled
distributions are statistically identical.
