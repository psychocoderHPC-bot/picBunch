\subsection{Bunch and grid configurations}
\label{sec:configs}

Every case measured in the paper is listed in
Table~\ref{tab:configs} with the bunch and the grid, and the section that
uses it. The entries are \emph{recorded} values transcribed from the run
artifacts: the mid-bunch slice cases from \texttt{B1} (bunch files
\texttt{bunches/gamma\{1,10\}-1k/bunch.h5}, read-back
\texttt{bunches\_verification.json} and the
\texttt{gamma\{1,10\}\_1k\_reference.h5/json} driver output) and
\texttt{LARGE1} (\texttt{data/plan.json}, \texttt{RESULTS.md}); the cost and
accuracy cases from \texttt{CFPORT} and \texttt{INT4}; the $\gamma$ landscape
from \texttt{ER1b} and the aspect-4 rows from \texttt{N2b}; the
multi-velocity cases from \texttt{MV2} and \texttt{MV1b}; and the divergence
case from \texttt{VAR1} (the \texttt{VAR1b} $N=200$ subset exists, but its
independent reference is pending, so no row is included). The generation line for the mid-bunch bunches is
\texttt{generate\_bunch.py --model gamma --gamma G --seed 42 --grid 64 64 32
--cell-size 1e-7 1e-7 1e-7 --centroid 32 32 16 --sigma 6 6 6 --weights
uniform} \citep{B1}. Entries that no artifact records are written as
``not recorded''; nothing here is inferred. The total charge is $N$ times
$-e$ for the electron bunches with one unit weight, where $e =
1.602176634\times10^{-19}$\,C; the kinetic energy is
$(\gamma-1)\,m_ec^2$ with $m_ec^2 = 510.999$ keV.

The mid-bunch bunches are mono-energetic: the read-back records
$\gamma_{\min}=\gamma_{\max}=1$ for the $\gamma=1$ bunch and
$\gamma_{\min}=\gamma_{\max}=10$ for the $\gamma=10$ bunch \citep{B1}.
The multi-velocity cases carry a 5\% energy spread and a $5^\circ$ angular
spread; \texttt{MV2} stores only the per-box sums of the error model, but its
bunch generator (the driver block at \texttt{02891fe6}) and the seeds
(100, 103) survive, so the per-particle sample and the adaptive bin edges
were regenerated and are shown in Fig.~\ref{fig:multivel}. The campaign
bunch files were unavailable, so the sample was reproduced from that
generator on \texttt{rosi\_v100} (the \texttt{jrc0880} route was down);
the LCG state and draw count are identical to the campaign, and only last-bit
\texttt{libm} differences between hosts are possible, so the sampled
distributions are statistically identical.
