 \section{Conclusion}
 \label{sec:conclusion}

 \texttt{picBunch} \texttt{v0.7.0} computes the present-time field of a
 uniformly moving, TSC-shaped bunch as a zero-padded free-space FFT convolution
 with staggered Yee read-out, driven by a single TOML configuration
 (Section~\ref{sec:interface}), and writes a uniform float32 checkpoint that
 restarts under a stock PIConGPU, periodic and open-boundary, single- and
 multi-patch. The release passes $18/18$ ctest with zero compiler warnings on
 a V100 \texttt{sm\_70}, an H100 \texttt{sm\_90}, and a gfx1100 HIP build
 \citep{INT4}.

 The opt-in near shell assembles exactly to roundoff, and its tile subtraction
  is not the limiting term; the seam excess at the fast/exact crossing is
  $2.03\times10^{-10}$ per component for a stationary single particle
  ($2.8\times10^{-10}$ for the $64$-source cloud) \citep{CFPORT,INT4}. The fast backend, an
 eight-term \texttt{asinh} box plus a plain Gauss--Legendre remainder, meets
 $10^{-3}$ stationary ($7.99\times10^{-4}$ interior) and moving axial
 ($1.69\times10^{-4}$ interior); the moving oblique interior is
 $8.15\times10^{-2}$, the measured limit of the fast backend
  \citep{CFPORT,INT4}. At $2\times10^{6}$ particles the mesh is
  $0.052$\,s on one H100, deterministic (md5 \texttt{d3641d64\ldots}); the
  near shell is $9.26$\,s support-only at $7.99\times10^{-4}$ and
  $42.6$\,s moving axial at $1.69\times10^{-4}$ (Section~\ref{sec:cost}).

 In the far field the mesh is below $3\times10^{-4}$ from $d=12$ cells for
 $\gamma=1$, aspect $1$, and from $d=32$ for $\gamma=1$, aspect $4$; at
 $\gamma=10$, aspect $1$, the measured values are $6.5\times10^{-3}$ at
 $d=32$ and $1.8\times10^{-3}$ at $d=48$ \citep{W4b,V2b}. The
 $\gamma$-landscape measurement shows the error growing steeply with
 $\gamma$ at a fixed distance in cells, with the beaming cone width
 $d/\gamma$ the controlling scale: at $d=8$ cells, $h=1$, the error is
$4.4\times10^{-2}$, $1.05$, $5.47$ for $\gamma=10,50,100$, and the
h-refinement exponent $p$ (error $\sim h^p$, fitted from the two spacings)
falls from $1.85$ to about $1.0$ as $\gamma$ rises
 (Section~\ref{sec:gamma-landscape}) \citep{ER1b}. The MPI compute is
 component-parallel mesh and tile-parallel near shell with one all-reduce each,
 bitwise identical at $1/2/4$ ranks, with the replicated deposit and source FFT
 capping the mesh speedup and the collective writer I/O-bound at four ranks
 (Section~\ref{sec:mpi-scale}) \citep{INT3,MPI2}. The method is a far-field
 tool: cheap, deterministic (md5 \texttt{d3641d64\ldots}), and portable across
 the three backends; the measured in-support error at $\gamma\ge50$ is
 reported in Section~\ref{sec:gamma-landscape}.
