\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\texttt{picBunch} \texttt{v0.5.0} computes the present-time field of a
uniformly moving, TSC-shaped bunch as a zero-padded free-space FFT convolution
with staggered Yee read-out, driven by a single TOML configuration
(Section~\ref{sec:interface}), and writes a uniform float32 checkpoint that
restarts under a stock PIConGPU. In floored relative per-component error
against its TSC-convolved target, the mesh is below $3\times10^{-4}$ from
$d=12$ cells for $\gamma=1$, aspect 1, and from $d=32$ for $\gamma=1$, aspect
4; at $\gamma=10$ it is $6.5\times10^{-3}$ at $d=32$ and $1.8\times10^{-3}$
at $d=48$, not below $3\times10^{-4}$ within $48$ cells \citep{W4b,V2b}. The
disagreement with the PIConGPU reference field is per-source, local to the
TSC support, and independent of particle count. The mesh alone does not reach
$10^{-3}$ where the slices cut the bunch core ($0.12$--$0.41$ for
$\gamma=1$, $0.11$--$4.60$ for $\gamma=10$); the opt-in near shell
(Section~\ref{sec:nearsheet}) assembles exactly to roundoff, so the residual
at the measured radii is the mesh outside the shell, not the tile
subtraction \citep{NS4b}. The near-field cost ladder is
$5.4\times10^{-5}$\,s per pair for the ported stationary box8/rem8 evaluator,
$3.1\times10^{-4}$\,s out of support and $6.9\times10^{-3}$\,s in support for
the accurate moving evaluator, $1.62$\,s for the upstream adaptive settings
(Section~\ref{sec:cost}); the MPI chunked output is bitwise identical across
rank counts and the $6R$ far-tail switch is $1.87\times10^{-3}$ of the target
\citep{FIX1}. On a two-species periodic deck the field round-trips bitwise
through a real PIConGPU restart, first-step displacement exactly
$\beta c\,dt$ with $\beta = 0.9949874367$
(Section~\ref{sec:comparison}). The method is a far-field tool: cheap,
deterministic (md5 \texttt{d3641d64\ldots}), and GPU-portable, with the mesh
limitation inside the bunch core documented by the slices and the
perpendicular direction at $\gamma\ge10$ not converged at the natural cell
size.
