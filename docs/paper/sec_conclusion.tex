\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The method computes the present-time field of a
uniformly moving, TSC-shaped bunch as a zero-padded free-space FFT
convolution with staggered Yee read-out, driven by a single TOML
configuration, and writes a uniform float32 openPMD checkpoint that restarts
under a stock PIConGPU, periodic and open-boundary, single- and multi-patch.
The implementation is validated on V100 \texttt{sm\_70}, H100
\texttt{sm\_90}, and gfx1100 HIP hardware.

The method is a far-field tool. In the far field the mesh is below
$3\times10^{-4}$ of the TSC-convolved continuum from $d=8$ cells for
$\gamma=1$, aspect 1, and from $d=32$ for $\gamma=1$, aspect 4; at
$\gamma=10$, aspect 1, the measured values are $6.3\times10^{-3}$ at
$d=32$ and $2.6\times10^{-4}$ at $d=48$ (Table~\ref{tab:d48}, L2; the
$d=48$ entry is a V100 value not re-measured here). At
$2\times10^{6}$ particles the mesh-only wall is $0.500$\,s (the mesh stage
of the $R=1.5$ cost row, compute $0.083$\,s) on one H100 of
JURECA H100,
deterministic (md5 \texttt{d3641d64\ldots}), and the cost grows with the
particle count for the deposit (sub-linear below $\sim10^6$) and is cubic in
the padded grid for the FFT.
The opt-in near shell assembles exactly to roundoff and its fast backend
meets $10^{-3}$ stationary and moving axial, with the moving oblique
interior at $1.94\times10^{-3}$, the measured limit. The MPI compute is a
component-parallel mesh and a tile-parallel near shell, bitwise identical
at $1/2/4$ ranks on JURECA H100 (four H100 PCIe), with the
distributed FFT reaching a $3.73\times$ speedup and the total (mesh stage
plus writer) a $2.52\times$ speedup at four ranks; the BP5 writer scales
sub-linearly, to $1.30\times$ from one to four ranks. The measured sub-cell
limit at $\gamma\ge50$ is stated in
Section~\ref{sec:gamma-landscape}; in that regime the near shell is the
working tool.
