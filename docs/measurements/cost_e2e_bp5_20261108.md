# Cost-table and writer-inclusive e2e re-measurement (BP5 stack, 2026-11-08)

Measured on `jrc0880.jureca` (jureca-h100, four H100 PCIe 80 GB, `sm_90`),
exclusive allocation `dc-h100` (4 h from 20:13:54 UTC), one GPU job at a time
on GPU 0, all four GPUs idle before every row, 2 s `nvidia-smi` sampler in
every row. Stack: ADIOS2 2.12.1 + openPMD-api 0.17.1 (BP5 engine), HDF5
1.10.10, gcc 13.3.0, CUDA 13.4.92. All four GPUs report
`clocks.max.sm = 1755 MHz`, power limit 350 W.

Binaries under test:

- **final code** `e1ddfb5` (integration tip, v0.9.2 + BP5 finalize fix),
  H100 build sha256 `d6c964ae775dbc2f9c23cc66b81e201388284392fe54c09a3b271de48f4f8275`.
- **v0.9.1** `c0bf82c` rebuilt on this exact stack (ADIOS2 2.12.1 /
  openPMD 0.17.1 / CUDA 13.4.92, `sm_90`) for the A/B row, sha256
  `6948df58aec287564bcd21bcf474843043c24c85825c0d131e85224fb6fa010d`.

## E0 — clock / throttle probe (throttling refuted)

5 min, 2 s sampler: 60 s idle, then 64 sequential cost-mesh CLI runs
(~3.7 s each). Idle: 345 MHz, 52.1 W, 30 C. Load: 1755 MHz reached (the
max SM clock), 200-234 W, 60-63 C, throttle reason `0x0`. `nvidia-smi -q`:
`SW Power Cap`, `HW Slowdown`, `HW Thermal Slowdown` and `Applications
Clocks Setting` all `Not Active`; the cumulative `SW Power Capping`
(2,373,593,626 us) and `SW Thermal Slowdown` (376,761,614 us) counters are
past history, not an active state. **There is no clock cap during the
block; the earlier throttling hypothesis is refuted.**

## E1/E2 — cost rows R=1.5/3/4 (2e6 particles, centred 20-cell drawer)

Command family (ADIOS2-BP5 output `<series>.bp`):

    picBunch --input in/input_N2000000_cost.h5 --cell-size 2.5e-7 \
      --grid 66,68,66 --origin -5.75e-6,-6e-6,-5.75e-6 --velocity-bins 1 \
      --tolerance 1e-3 --shape tsc --output <series>.bp \
      --near-shell fast --near-shell-radius <1.5|3|4> --near-shell-tile 4 \
      --near-shell-fast-order 5

Input `input_N2000000_cost.h5` sha256
`6d102276d546de6c511ac16d2adfe9bd3513ad2793be7449f5e444c401e067a0`,
generated with the fixed `tools/e2e/gen_inputs.py` (`47f2436`: uniform(0,20)
cell indices, `unitSI = 2.5e-7` m). Printed
`(source,node,component)` counts confirm the centred geometry:

| radius | printed (s,n,c) | (s,n) pairs | protocol |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.5 | 2,592,138,624 = 6 x 432,023,104 | 432,023,104 | 1 warm-up + 3 timed repeats |
| 3 | 8,749,074,432 | 1,458,179,072 | 1 timed repeat, no warm-up |
| 4 | 15,969,868,800 | 2,661,644,800 | 1 timed repeat, no warm-up |

R=3/R=4 use the historical single-run protocol (a 500-2000 s run makes a
warm-up a duplicate measurement; the published rows are single runs);
stated explicitly because it differs from the E1 protocol. Measured pair
counts agree with the paper rate x wall to 0.3 %, so the centred drawer
reproduces the paper geometry at all three radii.

| row | wall (s) | near-shell (s) | published (s) | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R=1.5, final | **500.95** (IQR 0.41) | 498.24 | 281.0 / 278.28 | 1.80x |
| R=1.5, v0.9.1 on this stack | **353.47** | 352.30 | 281.0 | 1.26x |
| R=3, final | **1268.29** | 1265.62 | 955.4 | 1.33x |
| R=4, final | **1959.31** | 1956.61 | 1508.12 | 1.30x |

Measured per-pair rates 0.867 / 1.152 / 1.360e6 pairs/s (R=1.5/3/4) against
the published 1.55 / 1.61 / 1.76e6: per-pair penalties 1.79 / 1.40 / 1.29,
so the gap is concentrated at small R.

**A/B decomposition (R=1.5, same node, same input, same pair count,
1755 MHz in every busy sample):** the final code is
**1.42x** the v0.9.1 binary on this stack (498.24 / 352.30 = 1.414 on the
near-shell wall); a device-path regression of the newer code on CUDA 13.4.
The v0.9.1 binary on this stack is **1.26x** the published value
(353.47 / 281.0 = 1.26x on the end-to-end wall; 352.30 / 278.28 = 1.27x on
the near-shell wall); a host/stack difference (CUDA 13.4 / driver 610 vs
the unrecorded paper toolchain). Neither term is a clock effect.

## E4 — writer-inclusive e2e (N=2e6, BP5 vs the paper's HDF5)

Same command family with `--cell-size 1e-6`, `--grid <20|100>`,
`--origin 0,0,0`, input `input_N2000000.h5` sha256
`8136cb51c5ee1edabcb719311bbb924c6b06528b855f5445e96966778c8e8a4d`.
1 warm-up + 3 timed repeats. The writer-inclusive number is the CLI stage
sum `deposit+kernel+fft+readout+writer` (the harness wall additionally
includes ~2.5 s of process/IO setup).

| case | total median (ms) | vs paper | writer (ms) | writer share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20-cell, g10 | **67.50** (IQR 26.95) | paper 60.5, **+11.6 %** | 5.47 | **8.1 %** |
| 100-cell, g100 | **127.66** (IQR 2.60) | paper 147.9, **-13.7 %** | 24.05 | **18.8 %** |

- 20-cell: readout is **bimodal** per repeat (18.2 / 23.4 / 70.8 ms,
  P75/P25 = 2.26, just under the 2.5 flag); the slow repeat is the known
  OMP-over-unified-memory readout mode. Deposit is stable (36.2-36.6 ms);
  the two fast-mode repeats (62.0 / 67.5 ms) bracket the paper value.
- 100-cell: the whole difference vs the paper is the writer backend (BP5
  24.05 ms vs the paper's HDF5 53.8 ms); the compute stages agree
  (deposit 39.7 vs 38.9, fft 32.3 vs 31.3, readout 26.8 vs 21.4 ms).
