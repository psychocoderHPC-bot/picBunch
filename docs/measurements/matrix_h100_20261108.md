# Matrix re-measurement on the final ADIOS2 2.12.1 stack (2026-11-08)

The sweep behind Table `tab:matrix` re-measured on `jrc0880.jureca`
(jureca-h100, four H100 PCIe 80 GB, `sm_90`), exclusive allocation, one
job at a time on GPU 0 (`CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0`). All four GPUs idle
before and after every row; every under-load SM-clock sample at
1755 MHz (the 345 MHz samples are the idle state). Stack: ADIOS2 2.12.1
+ openPMD 0.17.1 (BP5 engine), CUDA 13.4.92, driver 610.57.04.

- Bench binary `block1-src/build/picBunchBench`, sha256
  `39fbc8acac50c0585402d6b42c3a4e3f525fe133d4ba0ba1314c6fa501ecbe3d`.
- Source tree matches `e1ddfb5+47f2436`: `tools/bench/bench.cpp` sha256
  `b9cff799f466bbb6900605a9af7859a0d675448fdba94cf8cb6004078134a3ec`;
  `tools/bench/summarize.py` sha256
  `562c4cd18475392f79fef98f67b57a305856bc6f1c2371e121bf397cea4c5214`.
  `plan_min_ms`/`plan_median_ms` are present in the matrix CSV and
  `plan_ms` in the raw CSV.
- Protocol (pre-registered in `tools/bench/README.md`, `cost-and-noise.md`
  P2): 2 untimed warm-ups + 9 timed repeats, padding 2, one process at a
  time, 2 s settle between rows, 2 s `nvidia-smi` sampler. Median + IQR +
  min/max; `plan_ms` reported separately. Gate = 2x the measured B0
  IQR/median ratio.

### Classification

- **12 of 14 rows are quotable** (IQR/median <= 12.4956 %).
- **2 rows are bimodal**: row 7 (`fft_ms` + `readout_ms`) and row 13
  (`readout_ms`). No sample was dropped; both mode totals are reported.
  They are not quotable as a single number under the protocol.
- **0 rows insufficient-survivors** (all rows kept >= 8 of 9 timed
  repeats; rows 0, 1, 2, 5, 6, 11, 12 dropped one outlier, the rest kept
  9).
- `plan_ms` medians are reported separately (2.4-10.2 ms across rows) and
  are not part of `total_*`, matching the tool's definition.

### Bimodal rows (mode totals, sum of stage cluster medians)

The bimodal total is the sum of the per-stage cluster medians after each
bimodal stage is split at its largest adjacent gap (the pre-registered
`summarize.py` rule; no sample dropped). The two modes are correlated: the
FFT and read-out of a given run both land in the same mode.

| 7 | 2e6 | 100 | 1 | 37.729 | 5.347 | 41.010 / 106.623 (3 / 6) | 21.692 / 81.979 (3 / 6) | **115.99** | **241.89** |

For derived ratios the paper uses the low mode, because the claim is about
the typical case; the high mode is reported next to it in the table and the
text.

## Value moves beyond the 2x gate

Compared with the previous H100 re-measurement (`r9/matrix.csv`), **no
quotable row moves beyond the 12.4956 % gate** (2x the gate is
24.9913 %; also none reach it); the largest change is row 8 at -6.51 %.
The bimodal rows move in their high mode (row 7: low +10.1 % / high
+129.6 %; row 13: low +61.6 % / high +110.2 %). Against the committed
V100 matrix every row differs by far more than the gate, which is
expected: that table is a host change (V100 -> H100), already labelled
host-specific in the paper.

## Protocol adherence and deviations

- One process at a time; 2 s settle before each row; pre/post GPU idle
  check (`util=0 %`, `mem=0 MiB`, empty compute-app list) passed for every
  row.
- 2 s sampler: every under-load SM-clock sample was 1755 MHz; no throttle
  reason other than the idle bit was sampled. No foreign compute PID in
  any sampler trace.
- No row was aborted or re-run (no clock drop, no foreign process).
- `clock_mhz` in the raw CSV is left empty by the bench; the external
  sampler trace (`logs/<row>.sampler.log`) is the clock record, as the
  README specifies.
- Bimodal rows were not cherry-picked or re-run to hide the second mode.
